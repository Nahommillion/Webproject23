◉ What is the wheel spinner for?

Use a wheel to make quick, fair choices in classrooms, giveaways, presentations, meetings, games and everyday decisions.

  • Pick a student to answer a question.
  • Choose a customer for a giveaway.
  • Select a lucky attendee during a presentation.
  • Randomize who speaks first in a meeting.
  • Choose which task to start with.
  • Pick who goes first in a game.
  • Let the wheel decide between alternatives.

◉ How to use the wheel spinner

Type entries in the panel, click the wheel or press Ctrl + Enter, then customize colours, sounds, graphics and spin time.

◉ Wheel features

A flexible wheel designed for games, classrooms, events and work.

  • Rich audio controls.
  • Multi-wheel organization.
  • Weighted entries.
  • Instant sharing.
  • Custom visuals and themes.
  • Large lists and imports.

◉ Is my data private?

Local settings stay on your device unless you choose to share or use a connected service. Avoid sensitive information on public wheels.

◉ Can I use the wheel in OBS or Streamlabs?

Yes. Use the public wheel as a browser source while keeping owner controls private.

◉ Is the wheel truly random?

Normal spins use browser cryptographic randomness. A private owner target intentionally selects the next winner.
