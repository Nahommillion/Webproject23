🎡 What is the wheel spinner for?

Use SpinWheel for random choices in classrooms, giveaways, presentations, meetings, games and everyday decisions.

  • Pick a student to answer.
  • Choose a giveaway winner.
  • Randomize meeting order.
  • Choose a task or game turn.
  • Decide between alternatives.

🧭 How to use the wheel spinner

Type entries on the right, click the wheel to spin, or press Ctrl + Enter. Customize colours, sounds, graphics and spin time at the right.
